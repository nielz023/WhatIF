Line prefixes: VO = narrator, MOM / DAD / BROTHER = his family, FRIEND / BULLY / SCIENTIST = other supporting voices.
Lines in *italics* are visual, sound and music direction, not for ElevenLabs.
HUD = the on-screen counter, top right, always visible: DAY · SPEED · REAL CLOCK. From the twist on day 2, a second line: AWAKE (his hours).

**THE RULE OF THIS VIDEO.** Every time he wakes up, everything about him is ten times faster than when he fell asleep. So every day he lives is ten times shorter for the rest of the world. Day 2 lasts about two and a half real hours, day 3 about fifteen minutes, day 4 ninety seconds, day 5 nine seconds, and so on. All of his days fit before Tuesday 09:00:00, the exact minute his little brother blows out his birthday candles. The HUD clock shows this: it runs normally on day 1, then crawls towards 09:00 but never reaches it. If he stays awake for about 27 of his hours from the moment he wakes up, the clock does reach 09:00. That's the hope, and the fight.

**HOW OTHER PEOPLE SOUND.** We always hear the world from his point of view. Day 1: normal. From day 2, other people's voices are a deep, slow rumble, and their words appear as subtitles on screen. Generate those lines in ElevenLabs as normal, then slow them down and pitch them down hard in the edit, under the subtitle. To them, his voice is a fast, high chirp.

**MIX RULE: the voice-over always comes first.** Music and sound effects never drown out the voice-over or the dialogue. Music 12 to 18 dB under the voice in quiet parts, effects 10 to 15 dB under the voice while someone talks. Big hits (the sonic boom, the truck) land in the gaps between lines. Only instrumental music. Final mix about -14 LUFS, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP. Trim pauses inside the voice clips to 0.4 s, except the [long pause] tags.

**RECURRING THEME.** A ticking clock motif (soft clicks plus a four-note celesta melody) belongs to the birthday candles and the whiteboard. It plays on day 1, at the twist on day 2, on day 5 and on day 10.
